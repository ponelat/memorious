journal

A quiet place for what happened. Text, photos, and voice — captured in seconds, kept forever, on your own devices.

what it believes

  • Append-only. Nothing is ever overwritten. The past stays put.
  • Local-first. Every device holds the whole journal and works offline.
  • No accounts. Trust is a secret you carry, not a login you rent.
  • Peer-to-peer. Your devices sync directly with each other. No cloud in the middle.
  • Nothing asked of you. No tags, no folders, no filing. One stream, newest first.

how it works

capture

A text box, a camera, a microphone. Whatever happened goes in and you get on with your day.

sync

Phone, laptop, desktop — each one a full peer. Pair once with a QR code; they find each other from then on.

find

Voice notes transcribe themselves. Photos are read for text. Search remembers everything so you don't have to.
